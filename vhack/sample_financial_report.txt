# SME Financial & Operational Risk Report

## 1. Downtime Cost Formula
The official formula for calculating "Total Downtime Cost" in this facility is:
Total Downtime Cost = (Lost Revenue/Hr + Idle Labor/Hr + Recovery Cost/Hr) × Downtime Duration (Hours)

## 2. Operational Cost Components
- **Lost Revenue/Hr**: $500.00 (Based on average production output of 50 units/hr at $10 profit per unit)
- **Idle Labor/Hr**: $150.00 (Fixed cost for 5 machine operators who cannot work during downtime)
- **Recovery Cost/Hr**: $75.00 (Energy surge costs and system recalibration overhead after a shutdown)

Total Base Hourly Rate = $725.00/hr

## 3. Maintenance Strategy Costs & Durations
- **Strategy A (Quick Fix / Patch):** 
  - Action: Basic repair or lubrication. 
  - Duration: 2 hours. 
  - Direct Material Cost: $200.
- **Strategy B (Standard Component Replacement):** 
  - Action: Replacing major components (Bearings, Seals, etc.). 
  - Duration: 6 hours. 
  - Direct Material Cost: $800.
- **Strategy C (Full Overhaul):** 
  - Action: Complete disassembly, cleaning, and replacement of all wear parts. 
  - Duration: 14 hours. 
  - Direct Material Cost: $2,500.

## 4. Financial Risk Thresholds
- Low Risk: Total cost < $2,000
- Medium Risk: Total cost $2,000 - $10,000
- High Risk: Total cost > $10,000
