# SOP-MAIN-04: Standard Operating Procedure for Unscheduled Maintenance & Recovery
## Version 4.2 | Effective: January 2026

### 1. MTTR (Mean Time To Repair) Benchmarks
Unscheduled downtime duration is estimated based on the identified failure tier:

| Failure Tier | Example Symptoms | Estimated MTTR (Hours) | Recovery Tier |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Minor)** | Lubrication, Loose Sensor | 1.5 - 2.5 | A |
| **Tier 2 (Moderate)** | Worn Bearing, Seal Leak | 4.0 - 8.0 | B |
| **Tier 3 (Major)** | Motor Burnout, Gearbox Failure | 12.0 - 24.0 | C |
| **Tier 4 (Critical)** | Structural Damage, PLC Failure | 48.0+ | D |

### 2. Recovery Cost Tiers (Fixed)
Recovery costs cover safety inspections, calibration, and energy surges during system restart.

- **Tier A (Basic Restart)**: $350.00
- **Tier B (Standard Calibration)**: $1,200.00
- **Tier C (Deep Clean & Validation)**: $2,800.00
- **Tier D (Full Line Recertification)**: $7,500.00

### 3. Component & Tooling Lead Times
- **Standard Bearings/Sensors**: In-stock (0 hours delay)
- **Specialized Motors**: 24-hour sourcing delay (add to MTTR if not on-hand)
- **Custom Fabricated Parts**: 5-7 days lead time.

### 4. Safety & Compliance
- **LOTO (Lock-Out Tag-Out)**: Mandatory 30-minute procedure before any repair (included in MTTR).
- **QA Pass**: All Tier 2+ repairs require 1 hour of QA testing before production resumes.
