# CONFIDENTIAL: Q1 2026 Manufacturing Financial Risk Report
## Facility: Regional Assembly Plant - Sector B

### 1. Revenue Impact (Lost Sales)
- **Direct Revenue per Unit**: $125.00
- **Average Profit Margin**: 8.0% ($10.00/unit)
- **Baseline Throughput**: 65 Units/Hour
- **Lost Sales Opportunity (LSO) Rate**: $650.00/Hour
  *(Calculated as Baseline Throughput × Profit Margin)*

### 2. Operational Burn Rate (Idle Labor & Overhead)
- **Direct Labor Cost (DLC)**: $160.00/Hour (4 Operators + 1 Team Lead)
- **Fixed Overhead (FO)**: $90.00/Hour (Electricity, Rent, Support Services)
- **Total Burn Rate (TBR)**: $250.00/Hour
  *(Calculated as DLC + FO)*

### 3. Supply Chain Penalties (Contractual)
- **Late Delivery Penalty**: $1,500.00 per 24-hour period of delay.
- **Stock-Out Surcharge**: $500.00 fixed cost for emergency parts sourcing if failure is catastrophic.

### 4. Machine Criticality Weights
Machines are categorized by their impact on the production line:
- **Bottleneck (Level 3)**: 1.5x Impact Multiplier (Stops entire line)
- **Semi-Critical (Level 2)**: 1.0x Impact Multiplier (Slows line by 50%)
- **Support (Level 1)**: 0.5x Impact Multiplier (Redundant equipment available)

### 5. Downtime Cost Formula (Executive Standard)
**Total Business Impact (TBI) = [(LSO + TBR) × Duration × Criticality Multiplier] + Recovery Costs + Penalties**
