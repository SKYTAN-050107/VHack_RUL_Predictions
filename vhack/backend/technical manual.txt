# Industrial Machinery Technical Manual (SME Resilience Edition)

## 1. Conveyor Belt A
- **Failure Mode: Bearing Wear**
  - Symptoms: High-frequency vibration, squealing noise.
  - Fix: Replace NSK-6205 bearings. Apply lithium grease every 500 hours.
- **Failure Mode: Belt Slippage**
  - Symptoms: Reduced throughput, heat at drive pulley.
  - Fix: Tighten tensioner bolts by 2 full turns. Replace belt if cracks exceed 3mm.

## 2. Motor Pump 03
- **Failure Mode: Cavitation**
  - Symptoms: "Gravel" noise, fluctuating output pressure.
  - Fix: Check intake valve for blockage. Clear debris from impeller.
- **Failure Mode: Seal Failure**
  - Symptoms: Fluid leakage at shaft, drop in efficiency.
  - Fix: Replace mechanical seal (Model P-12). Check shaft alignment.

## 3. Hydraulic Press
- **Failure Mode: Valve Sticking**
  - Symptoms: Erratic ram movement, slow return stroke.
  - Fix: Flush hydraulic system. Replace filter (10-micron).
- **Failure Mode: Cylinder Leakage**
  - Symptoms: Visual oil on ram, inability to hold pressure.
  - Fix: Replace O-ring kit (Part #HP-OR-99). Priority: High.
